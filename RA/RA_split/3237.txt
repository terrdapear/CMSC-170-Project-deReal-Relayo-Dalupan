 *Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand fifteen.*

 **REPUBLIC ACT No. 10854**

 AN ACT SEPARATING THE PADADA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL - MALINAO EXTENSION IN BARANGAY LOWER MALINAO, MUNICIPALITY OF PADADA, PROVINCE OF DAVAO DEL SUR FROM THE PADADA NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, CONVERTING IT INTO AN INDEPENDENT NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS MALINAO NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL OF ARTS AND TRADE AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 *Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:*

 **SECTION 1.** The Padada National High School Malinao Extension in Barangay Lower Malinao, Municipality of Padada, Province of Davao del Sur is hereby separated from the Padada National High School and converted into an independent national high school to be known as Malinao National High School of Arts and Trade.

 **SECTION 2.** All personnel, assets, liabilities and records of the Padada National High School Malinao Extension are hereby transferred to and absorbed by the Malinao National High School of Arts and Trade.

 **SECTION 3.** The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the Departments program the operationalization of the Malinao National High School of Arts and Trade, the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current years appropriation of the Padada National High School Malinao Extension. Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued operation of the school shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

 **SECTION 4.** The Secretary of Education shall issue rules and regulations necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

 **SECTION 5.** This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

 Approved,

 | (Sgd.) **FRANKLIN M. DRILON** President of the Senate | (Sgd.) **FELICIANO BELMONTE JR.** Speaker of the House of Representatives |

 **REPUBLIC ACT No. 10857**

 AN ACT ESTABLISHING MANGROVE CRAB SEED BANKS, NURSERIES AND GROW-OUT PRODUCTION FARMS IN THE MUNICIPALITIES OF VIRAC, BATO, BARAS, GIGMOTO, VIGA, PANGANIBAN, BAGAMANOC, SAN ANDRES, CARAMORAN AND PANDAN, PROVINCE OF CATANDUANES AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 *Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:*

 **SECTION 1.** There shall be established, under the supervision of the Department of Agriculture, mangrove crab seed banks, nurseries and grow-out production farms in the municipalities of Virac, Bato, Baras, Gigmoto, Viga, Panganiban, Bagamanoc, San Andres, Caramoran and Pandan, Province of Catanduanes.

 **SECTION 4.** The Secretary of Agriculture shall include in the Departments programs the establishment of the mangrove crab seed banks, nurseries and grow-out production farms, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act. The LGUs concerned shall set aside funds from any available local revenue to fund their establishment, supervision and monitoring.

 **SECTION 5.** This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.
