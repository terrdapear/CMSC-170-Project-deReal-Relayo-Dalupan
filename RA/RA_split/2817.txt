HIGHWAY	"Fourteenth Congress Second Regular Session

 *Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-eighth day of July, two thousand eight.*

 **[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 9687, August 04, 2009 ]**

 **AN ACT NAMING THE ROAD STARTING FROM THE BOUNDARY OF IRIGA CITY AND THE MUNICIPALITY OF BUHI, PASSING THROUGH BARANGAYS JUSTINA AND LOURDES AND ENDING AT THE MUNICIPAL HALL OF BUHI, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR AS THE MAXIMINO NOBLES SR. HIGHWAY**

 **Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines in Congress assembled:**

 **Section 1.** The road starting from the boundary of Iriga City and the Municipality of Buhi, passing through barangays Justina and Lourdes and ending at the Municipal Hall of Buhi, Province of Camarines Sur shall hereinafter be known as the Maximino Noble Sr. Highway.

 **Section 2.** The Department of Public Works and Highways shall issue the necessary rules, orders and circulars to implement the provision of this Act.

 **Section 3.** This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in a newspaper of general circulation.

 Approved,

 | (Sgd.) **JUAN PONCE ENRILE** President of Senate | (Sgd.) **PROSPERO C. NOGRALES** Speaker of the House of Representatives |

 | (Sgd.) **EMMA LIRIO-REYES** Secretary of Senate | (Sgd.) **MARILYN B. BARUA-YAP** Secretary General House of Representatives |

 Approved: AUG 04 2009

 **Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines in Congress assembled:**

 **Section 3.** The Secretary of Public Works and Highways shall include in the Department's program the operationalization of the Cagayan De Oro City First District Engineering Office, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

 **Section 4.** The relevant provisions of Sections 1 and 2 of Republic Act No. 9066, and all other laws, decrees, orders, rules and regulations are hereby modified or amended accordingly.

 **Section 5.** This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
