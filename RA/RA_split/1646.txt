 AN ACT ESTABLISHING AN AGRICULTURAL HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF LANGUYAN, PROVINCE OF TAWI-TAWI, TO BE KNOWN AS THE TAWI-TAWI WEST COAST AGRICULTURAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 **Section 1.** There shall be established an agricultural high school in the Municipality of Languyan, Province of Tawi-Tawi, to be known as the Tawi-Tawi West Coast Agricultural High School.
