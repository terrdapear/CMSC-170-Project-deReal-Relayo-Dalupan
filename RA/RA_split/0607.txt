 **An Act Providing for the Establishment of the Palompon General Hospital in the Province of Leyte, and Authorizing the Appropriation of Funds Therefor**

 *Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:*

 **Section 1.** There shall be established, under the direct supervision of the Director of Medical Services, a hospital in the Municipality of Palompon, Province of Leyte, to be known as the Palompon General Hospital.

 **Section 2.** There is hereby authorized to be appropriated out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated, the sum of eighty thousand pesos for the construction of the Palompon General Hospital and for its operation and maintenance: *Provided,* That this hospital shall have at least twenty-five beds.

 **Section 7.** This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

 Approved: June 18, 1960.
