 Section 54. *Municipal Ordinances Existing at the Time of the Approval of this Act.* - All municipal ordinances of the Municipality of Biñan existing at the time of the approval of this Act shall continue to be in force within the City of Biñan until the sangguniang panlungsod ordinance shall proclaim otherwise.

 **Section 55.** *Plebiscite.* - The City of Biñan shall acquire corporate existence upon the ratification of its creation by a majority of the votes cast by the qualified voters in a plebiscite to be conducted in the present Municipality of Biñan within thirty (30) days from the approval of this Act. The Commission on Elections shall conduct and supervise such plebiscite. The expenses for such plebiscite shall be borne by the Municipality of Biñan.

 **Section 56.** *Officials of the City of Biñan.* - The present elective officials of the Municipality of Biñan shall continue to exercise their powers and functions until such time that a new election is held and the duly-elected officials shall have already qualified and assumed their offices. Appointive officials and employees of the municipality shall likewise continue exercising their functions and duties and they shall be automatically absorbed by the city government of the City of Biñan.

 **Section 57.** *Succession Clause.* - The City of Biñan shall succeed to all the assets, properties, liabilities and obligations of the Municipality of Biñan.

 **Section 58.** *Election of Provincial Governor and Sangguniang Panlalawigan Members of the Province of Laguna.* - The qualified voters of the City of Biñan shall be qualified to vote and run for any elective position in the elections for provincial governor, provincial vice governor, sangguniang panlalawigan members and other elective officials for the Province of Laguna.

 **Section 59.** *Jurisdiction of the Province of Laguna.* - The City of Biñan shall, unless otherwise provided by law, continue to be under the jurisdiction of the Province of Laguna.

 **Section 60.** *Suspension of Increase in Rates of Local Taxes.* - No increase in the rates of local taxes shall be imposed by the City within the period of five (5) years from its acquisition of corporate existence.

 **Section 61.** *Representative District.* - Until otherwise provided by law, the City of Biñan shall continue to be a part of the First Congressional District of the Province of Laguna.

 **Section 62.** *Reservation.* - Nothing herein contained shall preclude the determination by the appropriate agency or forum of boundary disputes or cases involving questions of territorial jurisdiction between the City of Biñan and any of the adjoining local government units even after the effectivity of this Act.

 **Section 63.** *Applicability of Laws.* - The provisions of the Local Government Code of 1991, and other laws pertaining to the Province of Laguna and such laws as are applicable shall govern the City of Biñan insofar as they are not inconsistent with the provisions of this Act.

 **Section 64.** *Separability Clause.* - If, for any reason or reasons, any partial provisions of this Charter shall be held unconstitutional, invalid or inconsistent with the Local Government Code of 1991, other parts or provisions hereof which are not affected thereby shall continue to be in full force and effect.

 **Section 65.** *Effectivity.* - This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its complete publication in at least two (2) newspapers of general and local circulation.
