The Board shall appoint the two (2) prominent citizens from among a list of at least five (5) qualified persons in the Province of Tarlac, as recommended by the search committee, in consultation with the Chairperson of the CHED and the other members of the Board, based on the standards and qualifications for the position.

 The Presidents of the federation of faculty associations, the federation of student councils and the federation of alumni associations shall serve in the Board only for the duration of their respective terms of office, as set forth in their respective constitutions and bylaws.

 The two (2) prominent citizens shall serve for a term of two (2) years from the date of appointment.
