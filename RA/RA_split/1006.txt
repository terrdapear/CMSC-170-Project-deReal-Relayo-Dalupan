
 **Section 2.** *Qualification for the Position of Public Works District Engineer.* No person shall be appointed as Public Works District Engineer unless he is a competent registered civil engineer, graduate of an engineering school or college duly recognized by the Government, with at least ten years experience in the Bureau of Public Works, and possessing the required and appropriate civil service eligibility.

 **Section 3.** *Duties, Functions and Responsibilities of Heads of Public Works Engineering District and the Regional Supervisors.* The duties, functions and responsibilities of heads of public works engineering districts, as well as the three regional supervisors, shall be prescribed in detail in an administrative order or regulation, not inconsistent with this Act, to be issued by the Director of Public Works with the approval of the Department Head.

 **Section 4.** *Duties and Responsibilities of Heads of Public Works City Engineering Offices.* Except as specifically mentioned in Section one of this Act, the existing city engineers shall be responsible for public works within the city and shall be responsible to the Director of Public Works insofar as the administration of public works under the jurisdiction of the Bureau of Public Works is concerned. The duties, functions and responsibilities of city engineers shall be in accordance with the provisions of the city charters, if any, or shall be prescribed in detail in an administrative order or regulation, not inconsistent with this Act or the pertinent provisions of the city charter, to be issued by the Director of Public Works with the approval of the Department Head.

 **Section 5.** *Authority of Public Works District Engineers to Administer Oaths and Take Testimony.* The heads of public works engineering districts shall have authority to administer oaths in the transaction of official business.

 **Section 6.** *Transfer of Personnel, Appropriations, Equipment and Others.* As organized, pursuant to the provisions of this Act, all personnel, unexpended appropriations, records, supplies, materials, equipment and other properties of the Bureau of Public Works in all highway district engineering offices, city engineering offices and project offices affected are transferred to the public works district and city engineering offices created under this Act. The Public Works Division in Highway Division Engineering Offices shall be abolished and personnel, unexpended appropriations, records, supplies, materials, equipment and other properties of the Bureau of Public Works in said Highway Division Engineering Offices are hereby re-allocated to the different public works district engineering offices or to the Central Office as the Director of Public Works may direct. The powers, duties and functions heretofore exercised and performed by the ex-officio public works division engineer, ex-officio public works district engineer and ex-officio public works city engineer office personnel shall hereafter be exercised and this Act insofar as they are not inconsistent with the provisions hereof and the regulations that may be issued hereafter.

 **Section 7.** *Regulations.* The Director of Public Works, with the approval of the Department Head, shall issue such rules and regulations, as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this Act.

 **Section 8.** *Appropriations.* In order to carry out the purposes of this Act, the sum of one million two hundred thousand pesos, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated out of the General Funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated. Positions of all public works employees and personnel, including those of the district engineers and necessary subordinate employees in public works engineering district offices, as well as, the positions of the three regional supervisors herein created, and the operating expenditures of the Bureau of Public Works shall be carried in succeeding General Appropriations Acts.

 **Section 9.** *Laws Repealed or Amended.* All Acts, executive orders, administrative orders, proclamations, rules and regulations, or parts thereof, inconsistent with this Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

 **Section 10.** *Date of effectivity.* This Act shall take effect on the first day of July, nineteen hundred and sixty-four.

 Approved: June 19, 1965.
