 **Section 10**. If, after three years from the date of the acceptance of this temporary permit, the grantee shall have fulfilled the same, the bond aforesaid shall be cancelled by the Secretary of Public Works and Communications.

 **Section 11**. Acceptance of this temporary permit shall be given in writing within six months after approval of this Act.  **Section 12**.  **Section 13**. This temporary permit shall be subject to amendment, alteration, or repeal by the Congress of the Philippines when the public interest so requires and shall not be interpreted as an exclusive grant of the privilege herein provided for.

 **Section 14**. This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

 Approved: June 18, 1964.
