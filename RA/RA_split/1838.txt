 AN ACT ESTABLISHING A HIGH SCHOOL SEPARATE FROM THE GENERAL SANTOS CITY HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY FATIMA, GENERAL SANTOS CITY, TO BE KNOWN AS THE FATIMA HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 **Section 1.** There shall be established, under the supervision of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports, a high school separate from the General Santos City High School in Barangay Fatima, General Santos City, to be known as the Fatima High School.
