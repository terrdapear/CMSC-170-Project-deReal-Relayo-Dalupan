 **An Act Creating the Barrio of Curva in the Municipality of Medellin, Province of Cebu**

 *Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippine Congress Assembled:*

 **Section 1.** The sitio of Curva in the Municipality of Medellin, Province of Cebu, is converted into a barrio of said municipality to be known as the barrio of Curva.

 **Section 2.** This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

 Approved: June 18, 1961.
