 **Section 1.** There is hereby granted to the Consolidated Mills, Inc., its successors or assigns, a temporary permit to construct, maintain and operate in the City of Manila and at such places in the Philippines as the said company may select, subject to the approval of the Secretary of Public Works and Communications, private fixed point-to-point and land-based and the land mobile radio stations for the reception and transmission of wireless messages on radiotelegraphy or radiotelephony, each station to be provided with a transmitting apparatus and radio receiving apparatus.
