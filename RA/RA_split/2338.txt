 **[ REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8921, August 22, 2000 ]**

 **AN ACT CONVERTING THE TAMBONGON BARANGAY HIGH SCHOOL OF BARANGAY TAMBONGON, MUNICIPALITY OF PANTUKAN, COMPOSTELA VALLEY PROVINCE INTO A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL TO BE KNOWN AS THE TAMBONGON NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND PROVIDING APPROPRIATE FUNDS THEREFOR**

 **Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:**

 **Section 1.** The Tambongon Barangay High School of Barangay Tambongon, Municipality of Pantukan, Compostela Valley Province is hereby converted into a national high school to be known as the Tambongon National High School.

 **Section 2.** The Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

 **Section 3.** The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

 **Section 4.** This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

 Approved,

 | (Sgd.) **FRANKLIN M. DRILON** President of Senate | (Sgd.) **MANUEL B. VILLAR JR.** Speaker of the House of Representatives |
