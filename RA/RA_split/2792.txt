 **REPUBLIC ACT No. 9569 April 17, 2009**

 AN ACT ESTABLISHING A NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY BUNAWAN, CITY OF DAVAO TO BE KNOWN AS BERNARDINO B. BOSQUE NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 *Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines in Congress assembled:*

 **Section 1.** *Establishment of a National High School.* - There is hereby established, under the supervision of the Department of Education, a national high school in Barangay Bunawan, City of Davao, to be known as Bernardino B. Bosque National High School.

 **Section 2.** *Appropriations.*- The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the Department's program the operationalization of the Bernardino B. Bosque National High School, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

 **Section 3.** *Implementation.*- The Secretary of Education shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

 **Section 4.** *Effectivity.*- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the *Official Gazette.*

 Approved,
