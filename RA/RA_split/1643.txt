 AN ACT ESTABLISHING A HIGH SCHOOL IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF DIFFUN, PROVINCE OF QUIRINO, TO BE KNOWN AS THE DIFFUN HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 **Section 1.** There shall be established a high school in the Municipality of Diffun, Province of Quirino, to be known as the Diffun High School.
