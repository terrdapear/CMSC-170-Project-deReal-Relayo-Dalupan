
 *Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representative of the Philippines in Congress assembled:*

 **Section 1.** *Establishment of a National High School.* - There is hereby established, under the supervision of the Department of Education, a national high school in Barangay Panlayaan, West District, City of Sorsogon, Province of Sorsogon to be known as Panlayaan National High School.

 **Section 4.** *Effectivity.*- This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the *Official Gazette.*
