 RULE I TITLE, PURPOSE, AND CONSTRUCTION

 **Section 1.** *Short Title.* - These Revised Implementing Rules and Regulations (RIRR) shall be known as the 2024 Implementing Rules and Regulations of the National Cultural Heritage Act, Republic Act (R.A.) No. 10066, as amended by R.A. No. 11961, hereinafter referred to as the Rules.

 **Section 3.** *Construction.* - These Rules shall be construed and applied in accordance with, and in furtherance of, the policies and objectives of the Act.

 The provisions of these Rules, insofar as practicable, shall also be used to guide the interpretation and implementation of existing and future laws of local application and local ordinances that pertain to cultural heritage.

 In case of conflict or ambiguity, which may arise in the implementation of these Rules, the National Commission for Culture and the Arts (NCCA), hereinafter referred to as the Commission, shall issue the necessary clarification.
