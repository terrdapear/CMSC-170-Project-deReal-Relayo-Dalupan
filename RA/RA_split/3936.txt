 AN ACT INCREASING THE BED CAPACITY OF THE SOUTHERN TAGALOG REGIONAL HOSPITAL IN THE CITY OF BACOOR, PROVINCE OF CAVITE, FROM ONE HUNDRED (100) TO THREE HUNDRED (300) BEDS, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 **Section 1.** The authorized bed capacity of the Southern Tagalog Regional Hospital in the City of Bacoor, Province of Cavite, shall be increased from one hundred (100) to three (hundred (300) beds.

 **Section 2.** The existing professional healthcare services and facilities of the Southern Tagalog Regional Hospital shall also be upgraded to conform with and be commensurate to the increase in bed capacity as provided in Section 1 hereof.

 **Section 3.** The existing workforce of the Southern Tagalog Regional Hospital, which includes doctors, nurses, and support personnel, shall be increased correspondingly.

 **Section 4.** The Secretary of Health shall, after consultation with the Medical Center Chief of the Southern Tagalog Regional Hospital, and in coordination with the Secretary of Budget and Management and the Chairperson of the Civil Service Commission, determine the additional new positions to be created.

 **Section 5.** The Secretary of Health shall immediately include in the Department's program the implementation of this Act, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

 **Section 6.** If any provision of this Act is declared unconstitutional, the remaining parts of provisions not affected shall remain in full force and effect.

 **Section 7.** All laws, decrees, executive orders, issuances, and rules and regulations contrary to or inconsistent with the provisions of this Act are hereby repealed or amended accordingly.

 **Section 8.** This Act shall take effect after fifteen (15) days following the completion of its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

 Approved,

 AN ACT AMENDING SECTIONS 22, 24, 25, 27, 28, 32, 34, 38, 39, 42, 51, 52, 56, 57, 127, 149, 174, 176, 179, 190, 199, AND 259 OF REPUBLIC ACT NO. 8424, OTHERWISE KNOWN AS THE NATIONAL INTERNAL REVENUE CODE OF 1997, AS AMENDED, AND FOR OTHER PURPOSE

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 **Section 1.** *Short Title.* - This Act shall be known as the ""Capital Markets Efficiency Promotion Act"".

 **Section 2.** *Declaration of Policy.* - The financial sector plays a significant role in the long-term growth of the national economy. A key policy consideration is to allow capital markets to develop as efficiently as possible, with the least intervention. The optimal taxation of capital markets, and the products and transactions that come with them, is an essential element in developing capital markets. Towards this end, the State recognizes the necessity of a simpler, fairer, more efficient, and regionally competitive passive income tax system to encourage savings, as well as develop and deepen capital markets.

 In the same vein, the State recognizes tax administration as a driver of economic growth, and the equitable taxation of similar products as an essential aspect of progressive taxation. By making tax compliance easier and more equitable, progressivity is ensured, tax effort is improved, discretion is eliminated, and trust in the taxation system is enhanced. Accordingly, the State adopts the following policies:

 (a) The State shall endeavor to simplify an otherwise complex tax system for easy compliance; (b) The State shall ensure that the taxation of passive income is equitable across all stakeholders and discourages arbitrage opportunities; (c) The State shall promote the development and tax competitiveness of capital markets within the context of globalization, increase capital mobility, and financial inclusion; (d) The State shall promote and incentivize investment in the trading of equity and debt securities; and (e) The State shall aid in the capacity of corporate entities to raise capital through capital markets. **Section 3.** Section 22 of the National Internal Revenue Code of 1997, as amended, is hereby further amended to read as follows:
