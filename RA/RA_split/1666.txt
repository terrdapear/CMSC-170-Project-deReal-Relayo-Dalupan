 **AN ACT GRANTING CEBU AIR, INC., A FRANCHISE TO ESTABLISH, OPERATE AND MAINTAIN TRANSPORT SERVICES FOR THE CARRIAGE OF PASSENGERS, MAIL, GOODS AND PROPERTY BY AIR, BOTH DOMESTIC AND INTERNATIONAL, WITH CEBU AS ITS BASE**
