 **Section 9.** The grantee shall post a bond in the amount of ten thousand pesos to answer for the fulfillment of the terms embodied in the franchise. If after two years from the date of the acceptance of the franchise the grantee shall have complied with the terms thereof, the Secretary of Public Works and Communications shall cause the cancellation of the aforesaid bond.
