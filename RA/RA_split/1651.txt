 AN ACT ESTABLISHING A VOCATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY DAHAT, MUNICIPALITY OF LAGONOY, PROVINCE OF CAMARINES SUR, TO BE KNOWN AS THE DAHAT VOCATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 **Section 1.** There shall be established a vocational high school in Barangay Dahat, Municipality of Lagonoy, Province of Camarines Sur, to be known as the Dahat Vocational High School.

 **Section 2.** The Secretary of the Department of Education, Culture and Sports shall issue such rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

 **Section 4.** This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
