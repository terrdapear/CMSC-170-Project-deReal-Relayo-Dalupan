 *Begun and held in Metro Manila, on Monday, the twenty-seventh day of July, two thousand fifteen.*

 **REPUBLIC ACT No. 10728**

 AN ACT SEPARATING THE TUKURAN TECHNICALVOCATIONAL HIGH SCHOOLTABUAN ANNEX IN BARANGAY TABUAN, MUNICIPALITY OF TUKURAN, PROVINCE OF ZAMBOANGA DEL SUR FROM THE TUKURAN TECHNICALVOCATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, CONVERTING IT INTO AN INDEPENDENT NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL, TO BE KNOWN AS TABUAN NATIONAL HIGH SCHOOL AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 *Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:*

 **SECTION 1.** *The Tukuran Technical-Vocational High School Tabuan Annex* in Barangay Tabuan, Municipality of Tukuran, Province of Zamboanga del Sur is hereby separated from the Tukuran Technical-Vocational High School and converted into an independent national high school to be known as Tabuan National High School.

 **SECTION 2.** All personnel, assets, liabilities and records of the Tukuran Technical-Vocational High School Tabuan Annex are hereby transferred to and absorbed by the Tabuan National High School.

 **SECTION 3.** The Secretary of Education shall immediately include in the Departments program the operationalization of the Tabuan National High School, the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current years appropriation of the Tukuran Technical-Vocational High School Tabuan Annex. Thereafter, the amount necessary for the continued operation of the school shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

 **SECTION 4.** The Secretary of Education shall issue rules and regulations necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act.

 **SECTION 5.** This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

 Approved,

 | (Sgd.) **FRANKLIN M. DRILON** President of the Senate | (Sgd.) **FELICIANO BELMONTE JR.** Speaker of the House of Representatives |
