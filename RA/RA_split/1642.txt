 AN ACT ESTABLISHING A HIGH SCHOOL IN BARANGAY TEBA, MUNICIPALITY OF MATANOG, PROVINCE OF MAGUINDANAO, TO BE KNOWN AS THE MATANOG HIGH SCHOOL, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 **Section 1.** There shall be established a high school in Barangay Teba, Municipality of Matanog, Province of Maguindanao, to be known as the Matanog High School.
