 Be if enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 **Section 1.** *Establishment of a National High School.* - There is hereby established, under the supervision of the Department of Education, a national high school in Barangay Palanas, Municipality of Pilar, Province of Sorsogon to be known as ***Palanas National High School.***
