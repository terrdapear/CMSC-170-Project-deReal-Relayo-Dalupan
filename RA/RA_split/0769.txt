 **An Act to Provide for Two School Divisions in the Province of Quezon**

 **Section 3.** Such sums as may be necessary to carry out the purpose of this Act for the school year 1961-1962 is hereby authorized, to be appropriated out of any funds in the National Treasury not otherwise appropriated.  **Section 4.** This Act shall take effect upon its approval.
