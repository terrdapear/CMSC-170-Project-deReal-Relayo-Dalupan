 AN ACT UPGRADING THE OFFICE OF THE LAND TRANSPORTATION OFFICE (LTO) IN THE CITY OF BAGUIO INTO A CLASS ""A"" DISTRICT OFFICE, PROVIDING POSITIONS THERETO, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 **Section 1.** The district office of the Land Transportation Office (LTO) in the City of Baguion is hereby converted into a Class ""A"" district office, providing the appropriate personnel for its operation.

 **Section 2.** The Secretary of Transportation shall immediately include in the Department's program the operationalization of the Department's program the operationalization of the LTO Class ""A"" District Office in the City of Baguio, the initial funding of which shall be charged against the current year's appropriation of the said office. Thereafter, the funding of which shall be included in the annual General Appropriations Act.

 **Section 3.** This Act shall take effect fifteen (15) days after its publication in the Official Gazette or in a newspaper of general circulation.

 Approved,
