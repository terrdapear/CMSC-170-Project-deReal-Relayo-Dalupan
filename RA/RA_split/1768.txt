
 AN ACT CONVERTING THE RURAL HEALTH UNIT IN THE MUNICIPALITY OF GUINSILIBAN, PROVINCE OF CAMIGUIN, INTO A FIVE-BED CAPACITY HEALTH INFIRMARY, TO BE KNOWN AS THE GUINSILIBAN HEALTH INFIRMARY, AND APPROPRIATING FUNDS THEREFOR

 Be it enacted by the Senate and House of Representatives of the Philippines in Congress assembled:

 **Section 1.** The rural health unit in the Municipality of Guinsiliban, Province of Camiguin, is hereby converted into a five-bed capacity health infirmary, to be known as the Guinsiliban Health Infirmary.

 **Section 2.** The amount necessary to carry out the provisions of this Act shall be included in the General Appropriations Act of the year following its enactment into law and thereafter.

 **Section 3.** This Act shall take effect upon its approval.

 Approved,
